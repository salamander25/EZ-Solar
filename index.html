ABOUT THE PROGRAM

How does a Pudu pilot work?

We start with a site walk and map the rows, charging zones, and safety protocols. Then we deploy a pilot fleet, train your team, and deliver measurable output gains.

You keep your O&M plan—robots simply automate the routine work and surface issues faster.

Where can Pudu robots operate?
Utility-scale farms, C&I rooftops, and storage-adjacent sites. We calibrate for terrain, slopes, and safety zones.
Do we need new infrastructure?
Minimal. We add charging docks, connectivity, and safe paths without changing existing power equipment.
How fast can we launch?
Most pilots deploy in 4–6 weeks, including mapping, training, and integration with your reporting stack.
